      How it works

      This is a Dublin-themed clone of PortSimEnv, an environment for planning who docks where and when (and with how many cranes) after the published plan has broken.

      • A plan entry is a ship, a berthing hour, a section (or named berth), and an optional crane count.
      • Ships may not overlap each other, closed sections, or a ship already alongside, and they cannot berth before they arrive.
      • On DFT tasks the three official Liebherr gantries are a crane pool. Hours alongside are ceil(workload / cranes).
      • Gale notices and tidal windows block berthing and departure. A ship that finishes cargo inside a window waits alongside until it ends. Transit needs official depth + tide − 1.0 m UKC; lying alongside needs survey + tide − 0.5 m.
      • Cost is section-hours of delay past each due time, plus 5 for moving a ship off its planned berth. An infeasible plan scores 0.2 × the share of clean ships. A feasible plan scores 0.2 + 0.8 × exp(−gap / 0.5) against a heuristic best-known cost, not a proven CP-SAT optimum.

      Sources and assumptions

      Verified facts:

      • Dublin Port Notice to Mariners 12.4, 8 August 2025 (Harbour Master Captain Michael McKenna): berth lengths, design/survey/approach depths, channel reaches, UKC 1.0 m manoeuvring / 0.5 m alongside.
      • Dublin Port Tide Tables 2026, North Wall (Poltips-4, National Oceanography Centre): HAT 4.50, MHWS 4.10, MHWN 3.40, MSL 2.41, MLWN 1.50, MLWS 0.70, LAT −0.10 m CD; semi-diurnal.
      • Tom Clarke (East Link) Bridge notice: 31.5 m opening span, 6.35 m clearance above CD (1.85 m at HAT).
      • Operator and berth snapshot: Irish Ferries 49 & 51A, Stena 51, DFT 50A/50N/50S (ICG: 480 m, three Liebherr 40 t gantries), MTL 41–45, oil berths OB1–OB4, Alexandra Basin RoRo jetty 26/27 (Harbour Master 2026: ~195 m Liverpool ferries, design 240 m).
      • Published ferry dimensions: Ulysses 209 m / 6.4 m; W.B. Yeats 194.8 m / 6.7 m; Stena Estrid 215 m; Stena Adventurer 210.8 m / 6.3 m.

      Assumptions (not official schedules): synthetic weekly calls; 40 m planning sections; 20 m overhang allowance on named RoRo berths; DFT crane rate 25 moves/hour; passenger RoRo 2 h / freight RoRo 6 h handling; harmonic tide (12.42 h) rather than a named day’s official prediction; MTL 41–45 planned as one continuous face; heuristic best-known costs. Full notes are in the project README.
